The handoff.

Plans come in as rules, not rows. The phone does the scheduling.

Goal→ ChatGPT→ Paste→ Notifications→ Adjust
01The prompt fais-moi writes for you
Create my notification plan in fais-moi format. Goal: Manage my PCOS and eat better. Length: 30 days from Mon 28 Sep 2026. My day: awake 06:30–22:30, work 07:00–16:00. Limit: no more than 6 reminders a day. Also: meal prep on Saturdays, Bible affirmations at night, gentle tone. Rules – Reply with ONE json code block, nothing else. – Follow the schema below exactly. – Write a different message for each day a reminder fires, up to 30, so none feel stale. – Messages under 90 characters. 24-hour times. [fais-moi format v1 schema — attached automatically]

03What fais-moi does with it
Validate
✓ JSON, fais-moi format v1 — 7 notifs, 165 messages ✓ Every time sits inside waking hours ! Saturday has 7 notifs, limit is 6 → Hydration moved to weekdays ✓ Unknown fields ignored, missing icons defaulted
Not JSON? A forgiving reader picks up lines like “8:00 AM — Breakfast: …”. Anything it can't place is flagged for review, never guessed quietly.
Schedule Rules expand on the phone into dated notifications. Day 12 of Breakfast gets message 12. Only the next ~60 are handed to the OS at once; the rest are topped up whenever the app wakes.
