Sure! Here's a gentle evening routine to help you wind down. I kept it simple so it's easy to follow.

**Evening routine**

- 7:30 PM — Dinner: Eat slowly, no screens at the table.
- 8:30 PM — Tidy up: Ten-minute reset of the kitchen and living room.
- 9:15 PM — Read: A few pages of something that isn't work.
- Evening wind-down: Lights low, phone on the charger.

Stick with it for a couple of weeks and you'll notice the difference. Good luck! 🌙
